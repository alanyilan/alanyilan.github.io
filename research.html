Current projects

  • Sustainability promotions in the food-away-from-home industry

    The industry promotes different aspects of sustainability in different ways. How do consumers react to different sustainability goals, and to the measures used to promote them? We use discrete choice experiments to learn how people make sustainable choices in the food industry.

    Survey data collected. One published paper and four working papers, three of them under review.

  • Sustainable agricultural production

    Efforts to improve sustainability in specialty crop production include developing low-carbon technologies. We show that consumer premiums exist for various technologies and different levels of carbon reduction.

    Survey data collected. Three working papers, two under review. One field experiment under design.

  • Food waste reduction

    Food waste reduction happens at many stages of the food system. We use a survey and a field experiment to learn how consumers and farmers regard these efforts.

    Survey data collected. One working paper and one field experiment planned.

  • Willingness to pay for forestry ecosystem services

    Forests provide a range of ecosystem services. Understanding how forest users value those services, and how information shifts that valuation, is central to managing them.

    Survey data under collection. Two working papers, one under review.

  • Educational equity

    An online survey targeting minority students in the United States, examining how they choose among short-term online educational programs.

    Survey data collected. Two working papers, one under review.

  • Economic implications of water quality

    Harmful algal blooms generate negative externalities across many margins, including beach usage and fishing trip decisions.

    Survey data collected. Two working papers.

  • Kansas water conservation

    Kansas communities adopt water conservation strategies for different reasons and at different stages. We are working to identify the key drivers of residents' motivation and to translate that experience to broader communities.

    Grant funding secured.
